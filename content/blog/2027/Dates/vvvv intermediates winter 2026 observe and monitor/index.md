In this session, [Seb Escudié](https://thenodeinstitute.org/instructor/sebescudie?utm_source=vvvv&utm_medium=blog) shows you how to monitor GPU, memory and disk usage across all machines of a vvvv installation. The topics range from the core concepts of observability and running Prometheus and Grafana locally in Docker to instrumenting a vvvv patch with VL.Prometheus and building one dashboard for a whole multi-client setup. By the end, you have a working monitoring stack and the foundation to add it to your own installations, whether they run on one machine or twenty.

The session takes place on **February 9th** (Tuesday), **18:00 to 21:00 CET**. It runs live and online, and the recording stays available for at least a year afterwards.

## About the instructor

**Seb Escudié** is a Paris-based freelancer and long-time vvvv user, working on the back-end side of large-scale multimedia installations. Seb has worked with Refik Anadol Studio for years, most recently on Dataland, the first museum of AI arts in Los Angeles, and maintains the public Prometheus integration for vvvv.
