In three sessions, three instructors each build a real app from a blank patch: a camera **photobooth** with MediaPipe hand triggers and a state machine at its heart, a **quiz** whose content lives in a CMS instead of the patch, and a **desktop utility** with an ImGui interface that ships with an installer. Each one starts naive, runs into real walls, and introduces the idea that gets it unstuck: Channels, Observables, state machines and immutable data.

All three sessions take place on **January 12th, 19th and 26th** (Tuesdays), **18:00 to 21:00 CET**. They run live and online, and the recordings stay available for at least a year afterwards.

Sign up here: **[Let's Build an App Together](https://thenodeinstitute.org/courses/lets-build-an-app-together-development-patterns-for-real-projects-in-vvvv?utm_source=vvvv&utm_medium=blog)**

## About the instructors

- **[Randall Vásquez](https://thenodeinstitute.org/instructor/randall-vasquez?utm_source=vvvv&utm_medium=blog)** is a systems engineer with 20+ years in software and code running inside museum and VR installations with Marshmallow Laser Feast, and has maintained VL.OpenCV since 2017.
- **[Sebastian Huber](https://thenodeinstitute.org/instructor/huberschnellebuntebilder-de?utm_source=vvvv&utm_medium=blog)** co-founded [schnellebuntebilder](https://schnellebuntebilder.de), building museum exhibitions, immersive installations and audiovisual performances, and is active in many open-source projects.
- **[Seb Escudié](https://thenodeinstitute.org/instructor/sebescudie?utm_source=vvvv&utm_medium=blog)** is a creative coder and back-end developer who contributes to VL.Elementa and has wrapped many libraries and APIs for vvvv gamma.
