In two sessions, [Irwin Quemener](https://thenodeinstitute.org/instructor/lecloneur?utm_source=vvvv&utm_medium=blog) takes you from using TextureFX, vvvv gamma's library of GPU texture operations, to writing your own. The topics range from feedback effects, texture-driven displacement of 3D geometry and color grading to authoring custom TextureFX in SDSL and using them for compositing, object placement and touch interaction. By the end, TextureFX is a flexible tool you can bring into your own generative, compositing and interactive work.

Both sessions take place on **December 1st and 2nd** (Tuesday and Wednesday), **16:00 to 19:00 CET**. They run live and online, and the recordings stay available for at least a year afterwards.

## About the instructor

**Irwin Quemener** aka *lecloneur* is the creative and technical lead and co-founder of [CloneProduction](https://www.cloneproduction.net), a Shanghai studio building permanent installations into architectural spaces. He co-authored the TextureFX+ system in VL.Addons, which brings contributions from across the vvvv community together in a single library.
