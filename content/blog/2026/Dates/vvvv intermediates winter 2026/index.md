The new intermediate program at The NODE Institute is out and **early bird registration is open until August 31st**. Seven courses across twelve sessions, from the end of October until February, for everyone who has the basics of vvvv gamma down and wants to go deeper. After August 31st the regular rates apply, and spots are limited on top of that, so better get your seat now.

All sessions take place on **Tuesdays, 18:00 to 21:00 CET**, live and online. Every one of them is recorded, so nothing is lost if you cannot make an evening.

## Intermediate Classes

This season covers a broad collection of topics - take all of it, or just the parts you need. Massive thanks to all the instructors for making this possible!

- **27 Oct + 3 Nov 2026**<br>
**[Advanced TextureFX Techniques in vvvv](https://thenodeinstitute.org/courses/advanced-texturefx-techniques-in-vvvv/)** by [Irwin Quemener](https://thenodeinstitute.org/instructor/lecloneur)<br>
From using TextureFX nodes to writing your own ones in SDSL - feedback-driven water, displaced terrain, color grading, and a fully interactive composition built from nothing but textures.

- **10 + 17 Nov 2026**<br>
**[Applied 2D & 3D Math for Creative Coders](https://thenodeinstitute.org/courses/applied-2d-3d-math-for-creative-coders/)** by [Toby Knyvett](https://thenodeinstitute.org/instructor/toby-knyvett)<br>
Vectors, matrices, quaternions and fields - patched yourself in Skia and Stride, until gimbal lock,  Signed Distance Fields and your own cameras stop being something to survive.

- **24 Nov 2026**<br>
**[Driving Particle Systems from Geospatial Data in vvvv](https://thenodeinstitute.org/courses/driving-particle-systems-from-geospatial-data-in-vvvv/)** by [Rosi Grillmair](https://thenodeinstitute.org/instructor/rosi-grillmair)<br>
Turn real datasets into living, GPU-driven particle art: color maps out of GIS software become the control signals for emission, size, color and forces in VL.Fuse.

- **8 + 15 Dec 2026**<br>
**[Pixels in Space: Projection Mapping & Immersive Setups in vvvv](https://thenodeinstitute.org/courses/pixels-in-space-projection-mapping-immersive-setups-in-vvvv/)** by [Simon Weckert](https://thenodeinstitute.org/instructor/dlvr)<br>
Step out of the 16:9 monitor and into the physical world - mapping onto objects, edge blending, multi-display setups and frame-accurate sync across networked machines.

- **12 + 19 + 26 Jan 2027**<br>
**[Let's Build an App Together: Development Patterns for Real Projects in vvvv](https://thenodeinstitute.org/courses/lets-build-an-app-together-development-patterns-for-real-projects-in-vvvv/)** by [Randall Vásquez](https://thenodeinstitute.org/instructor/randall-vasquez), [Sebastian Huber](https://thenodeinstitute.org/instructor/huberschnellebuntebilder-de) and [Seb Escudié](https://thenodeinstitute.org/instructor/sebescudie)<br>
Three instructors, three real apps from blank patches to shipped products. Each one starts naive, hits a wall, and introduces the architectural ideas that unlock them: state machines, channels, observables and immutable data.

- **2 Feb 2027**<br>
**[Scaling vvvv Projects: Team Workflows & Git Versioning](https://thenodeinstitute.org/courses/scaling-vvvv-projects-team-workflows-git-versioning/)** by [Randall Vásquez](https://thenodeinstitute.org/instructor/randall-vasquez)<br>
How to actually use git with patches: splitting and naming files so parallel work stays out of trouble, pinning dependencies, and repairing a deliberately broken project with VLMergeTool.

- **9 Feb 2027**<br>
**[Observe and Monitor your Multi-Client vvvv Installations](https://thenodeinstitute.org/courses/observe-and-monitor-your-multi-client-vvvv-installations/)** by [Seb Escudié](https://thenodeinstitute.org/instructor/sebescudie)<br>
Prometheus, exporters and Grafana around your installation, so you can finally answer "how hot is that GPU?" for every client machine at once - and spot the memory leak before the show does.

## Need the basics first?

The **[vvvv Beginner Class Part I: Playful Patching](https://thenodeinstitute.org/courses/vvvv-beginner-class-part1-winter-2026/)** with [Rosi Grillmair](https://thenodeinstitute.org/instructor/rosi-grillmair) and [Gabriela Gordillo](https://thenodeinstitute.org/instructor/999) runs Mondays from October 19th and takes you from zero to patching in eight sessions. It continues in January with **[Part II: Foundations of VL Development](https://thenodeinstitute.org/courses/vvvv-beginner-class-part2-winter-2026/)** by [Christoph Ignaz Kirmaier](https://vvvv.org/people/chk/), four sessions on object-oriented patching and structuring larger projects in VL. Both are running on early bird tickets as well.
