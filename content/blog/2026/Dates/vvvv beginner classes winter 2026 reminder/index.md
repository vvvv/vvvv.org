New to patching in vvvv and VL? Then this is for you!

The **vvvv Beginner Classes** at The NODE Institute start on **October 19th** and take you from opening vvvv gamma for the first time to building your own real-time systems in its own visual programming language.

- **[Part I: Playful Patching in vvvv gamma](https://thenodeinstitute.org/courses/vvvv-beginner-class-part1-winter-2026?utm_source=vvvv&utm_medium=blog)** by [Rosi Grillmair](https://thenodeinstitute.org/instructor/rosi-grillmair?utm_source=vvvv&utm_medium=blog) and [Gabriela Gordillo](https://thenodeinstitute.org/instructor/999?utm_source=vvvv&utm_medium=blog) - 8 sessions, from October 19th
- **[Part II: Foundations of VL Development](https://thenodeinstitute.org/courses/vvvv-beginner-class-part2-winter-2026?utm_source=vvvv&utm_medium=blog)** by [Christoph Ignaz Kirmaier](https://vvvv.org/people/chk?utm_source=vvvv&utm_medium=blog) - 4 sessions, from January 11th

All sessions take place on **Mondays, 18:00 to 21:00 CET**, live and online. Every session is recorded, so nothing is lost if you cannot make an evening.

## Who is it for?

- Artists who find inspiration in nature, animation and technology
- Technologists searching for a multipurpose toolkit for interactive installations
- Architects and designers who want new ways of visualising their ideas
- Developers who are annoyed by coding and want to try visual programming instead

## What do I need?

vvvv runs on **Windows** and **macOS** (via Parallels), see the [Downloads](/download) section for your options.

A 3-button mouse and a second monitor are recommended, and a reasonably capable graphics card helps. No prior programming or vvvv experience required, just curiosity and a willingness to experiment.

## Already know the basics?

Have a look at the **[vvvv Intermediates Program](https://thenodeinstitute.org/courses/vvvv-intermediates-bundle-winter-2026-27?utm_source=vvvv&utm_medium=blog)** instead: seven courses on Tuesdays from the end of October until February, on TextureFX, applied math, geospatial particles, projection mapping, app architecture, git workflows and monitoring.
