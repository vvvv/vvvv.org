In two sessions, [TobyK](https://thenodeinstitute.org/instructor/toby-knyvett?utm_source=vvvv&utm_medium=blog) takes you through vectors, matrices and quaternions step by step - until gimbal lock, signed distance fields and building your own cameras are no longer something to survive, but somewhere to play. By the end, you will understand the math behind everything you move around in Stride and Skia.

Both sessions take place on **November 10th and 17th** (Tuesdays), **18:00 to 21:00 CET**. They run live and online, and the recordings stay available for at least a year afterwards.

Sign up here: **[Applied 2D & 3D Math for Creative Coders](https://thenodeinstitute.org/courses/applied-2d-3d-math-for-creative-coders?utm_source=vvvv&utm_medium=blog)**

## About the instructor

**TobyK** is a software developer and interactive designer, and the author of the maths patches in [VL.ExtendedTutorials](https://github.com/TobyKLight/VL.ExtendedTutorials). In his own words: *"I actually wasn't born a maths prodigy, I think this makes me a better instructor because I worked it out step by step and I still remember the way up the mountain."*
