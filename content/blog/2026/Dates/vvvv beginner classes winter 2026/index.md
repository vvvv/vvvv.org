## Part I: Playful Patching in vvvv gamma

Join a diverse group of artists, designers, technologists, musicians, architects and curious beginners for an immersive introduction to computational design and visual programming. Over eight weeks you build the visual intuition, confidence and creative momentum that make real-time graphics feel natural. This first chapter is about exploration, playful experimentation and learning to think visually with the tool.

- **19 Oct 2026** - *Getting Started: Navigating the vvvv Environment* - Find your way around the interface, connect your first nodes and get something on screen in the first evening.

- **26 Oct 2026** - *Exploring Space: The Real-Time 3D Pipeline* - Geometry, transforms, lighting and cameras, and how they work together to bring an object to life.

- **2 Nov 2026** - *Visual Rhythm: Textures, Images & Audio-Reactive Graphics* - Bring in sound as a creative driver, the basis for audiovisual installations and live performance.

- **9 Nov 2026** - *Patterns in Motion: Iterating with Spreads & Lists* - The step from a single object to hundreds of them, and the one idea that makes generative work in vvvv possible.

- **16 Nov 2026** - *Generative Design I: Movement, Logic & Creative Algorithms* - Animate what you have built, and give it rhythm, variation and flow.

- **23 Nov 2026** - *Generative Design II: Systems, Behaviors & Emergence* - How simple rules and feedback loops grow into complex structures that you guide rather than animate by hand.

- **30 Nov 2026** - *Data Visualization: Turning Information into Visual Form* - Reading real sources, cleaning up what comes back and giving it a shape, for artistic and analytical projects alike.

- **7 Dec 2026** - *Shader Patching with VL.Fuse: GPU-Powered Particles, Fluids and more* - Your first move onto the graphics card, and the gateway into advanced real-time work.

## Who is it for?

- Artists who find inspiration in nature, animation and technology
- Technologists searching for a multipurpose toolkit for interactive installations
- Architects and designers who want new ways of visualising their ideas
- Developers who are annoyed by coding and want to try visual programming instead

## Part II: Foundations of VL Development

After eight weeks of exploration many learners reach a natural turning point. You start to recognise patterns and structures that call for deeper tools, and the questions change: how do I build larger systems, how do I structure logic, how do I turn sketches into something durable?

**[Part II: Foundations of VL Development](https://thenodeinstitute.org/courses/vvvv-beginner-class-part2-winter-2026?utm_source=vvvv&utm_medium=blog)** by [Christoph Ignaz Kirmaier](https://vvvv.org/people/chk?utm_source=vvvv&utm_medium=blog) is made for exactly that moment. Four sessions in January on the conceptual side of vvvv gamma: system thinking in VL, records and custom data types, classes and mutability, and applied logic for games and interactive systems. This is where patching becomes system design.

- **11 Jan 2027** - *System Thinking in VL*
- **18 Jan 2027** - *Records & Custom Data Types*
- **25 Jan 2027** - *Classes, Mutability & System Architecture*
- **1 Feb 2027** - *Applied Logic for Games & Interactive Systems*

When enrolling in Part I you can add Part II at a reduced rate, which gives you a coherent twelve-session path from visual exploration to system-level thinking.

## What do I need?

A Windows computer with the latest vvvv gamma installed, a 3-button mouse and a second monitor are recommended, and a reasonably capable graphics card helps. No prior programming or vvvv experience required, just curiosity and a willingness to experiment.
