In two sessions, [Simon Weckert](https://thenodeinstitute.org/instructor/dlvr?utm_source=vvvv&utm_medium=blog) takes you from the 16:9 monitor to real surfaces and whole rooms. The topics range from projection mapping onto physical objects, with edge blending, perspective correction and calibration, to multi-display setups, frame-accurate sync across networked machines and keeping large setups running smoothly. By the end, you have the technical foundation for interactive installations, stage designs and immersive exhibition rooms.

Both sessions take place on **December 8th and 15th** (Tuesdays), **18:00 to 21:00 CET**. They run live and online, and the recordings stay available for at least a year afterwards. A second monitor is required for this one, so have your HDMI and DisplayPort cables ready.

## About the instructor

**Simon Weckert** is a creative technologist specializing in large-scale projections and immersive vvvv setups. Together with Refik Anadol he turns data streams into immersive environments, exhibited at MoMA, Centre Pompidou and ZKM. More at [simonweckert.com](https://www.simonweckert.com).
