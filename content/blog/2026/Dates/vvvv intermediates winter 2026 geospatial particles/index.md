In this session, [Rosi Grillmair](https://thenodeinstitute.org/instructor/rosi-grillmair?utm_source=vvvv&utm_medium=blog) shows you how to drive a VL.Fuse particle system with real geospatial data. Color maps exported from GIS software become the control signals for emission, size, color and the forces acting on your particles, and every dataset you layer on top grows the piece into a living, generative composition. You leave with your own data-driven particle system, and a working pipeline for any dataset you can turn into a texture.

The session takes place on **November 24th** (Tuesday), **18:00 to 21:00 CET**. It runs live and online, and the recording stays available for at least a year afterwards.

## About the instructor

**Rosi Grillmair** is an artist, creative coder and educator working with real-time processing, digital fabrication and data visualization, and has been teaching vvvv at The NODE Institute since 2022. Her works were shown at Ars Electronica, TADAEX Tehran, Medialab Prado and the Gwangju Media Arts Festival. More at [rgba.studio](https://www.rgba.studio/).
