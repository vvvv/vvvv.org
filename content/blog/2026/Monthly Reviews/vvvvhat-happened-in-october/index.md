Oooops, 

did anyone miss the revvvview yet? Sorry for late this time, let's see what October has brought us!

I can tell you this: We're mostly feature complete with the upcomming 8.0 release. Latest additions:
- [Final improvements for Packman](https://vvvv.org/blog/2026/update-improvements-for-the-package-manager/)
- [Boygrouping is back](https://vvvv.org/blog/2026/introducing-distributed-rendering-aka-boygrouping/)

And we have the following in the final touches:
- A revamped integration of [Rive](https://vvvv.org/blog/2026/introducing-distributed-rendering-aka-boygrouping/) 
- Full support for [Orbbec](https://www.orbbec.com/) Depth Cameras based on their 2.0 SDK
- Support for markerless motion capture by [MOVIN](https://www.movin3d.com/)

Also well under way is work on a new file format for VL documents: More compact, git-friendly, human readable. More on that soon...
