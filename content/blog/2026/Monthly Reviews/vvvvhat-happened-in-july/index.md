So, what happened?

Well...

- [vvvv gamma 7.4](https://thegraybook.vvvv.org/changelog/7.x.html) is out!
- We have the first previews out for upcomming vvvv gamma 8.0, including the long promised package manager. Read all about it here: [Introducing: A package manager for VL Packs](https://vvvv.org/blog/2026/introducing-a-package-manager-for-vl-packs/)
- [VL.DollarsMoCap](https://www.nuget.org/packages/VL.DollarsMoCap) is out with an update to now support up to 5 skeletons!
- [The 35th vvvv worldwide meetup](https://www.youtube.com/live/-0ElGQv9baQ) happened and is still available for you to rewatch.
- There've been 2 vvvv related talks at [NODE Session IV](https://thenodeinstitute.org/event/node-session-iv-creative-coding-event-in-berlin-the-node-institute):
  - [Randall Vasquez](https://youtu.be/X-2NcrdkaIs?t=5401) on his journey with vvvv
  - [Wolf Moritz Cramer](https://youtu.be/X-2NcrdkaIs?t=6922) on his new contribution [VL.Rhino](https://wolfmoritzcramer.de/vl-rhino/)

## Learning vvvv

[The NODE Institute's](https://thenodeinstitute.org/) winter season of **[10 vvvv courses](https://youtu.be/X-2NcrdkaIs?t=6213)** has been announced.
