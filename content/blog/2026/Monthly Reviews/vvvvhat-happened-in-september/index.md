So...

...vvvv had a great time at Ars Electronica. Thanks for everyone popping by and if you've missed it, you can re-watch the [Ars Edition vvvv meetup](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cUvYqkO2kFc&t=1443s&pp=ygUPdnZ2diBtZWV0dXAgYXJz).

Let's see what else: We're focused on ironing out final issues with [Packman](https://vvvv.org/blog/2026/introducing-a-package-manager-for-vl-packs/) and the still to be announced new Boygrouping feature for a clean 8.0 release. But in parallel work is already well under way in branches for 8.x that might revolutionize the way we collaborate with the machines. More on that later...

## Learning vvvv

October starts the winter season of vvvv courses hosted by [The NODE Institute](https://thenodeinstitute.org/):

- [Beginners: Playful Patching in vvv gamma](https://thenodeinstitute.org/courses/vvvv-beginner-class-part1-winter-2026?utm_source=vvvv&utm_medium=blog) from October 19

- [Advanced TextureFX Techniques](https://thenodeinstitute.org/courses/advanced-texturefx-techniques-in-vvvv/?v=5f02f0889301) October 27 and November 3

And here's a recent 3 part free online course by Carlo Gioia of [AVENUE CoVE](https://www.avenuecove.eu/):
1) [vvvv gamma Fundamentals: Real-Time Visual Programming](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TIuMqy0Gx1o)
2) [From Peripherals to Motion Tracking](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktiNuTU0D78)
3) [Projection Mapping to Stage Lighting](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zg-DfgZhRHw)
