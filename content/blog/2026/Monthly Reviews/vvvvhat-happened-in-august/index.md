Here goes..

A bit special interest, but vvvv finally supports [stereoscopic rendering](https://vvvv.org/blog/2026/introducing-support-for-stereoscopic-rendering/), which is really cool if you have the required hardware at hand.

And even more special interest, triggered by the above, we've released [VL.DeepSpace](https://vvvv.org/blog/2026/introducing-vl.deepspace/). A pack to get you started developing applications for the Ars Electronica Deep Space.

In our quest to offer support for a wide range of motion capture systems for avateering, we've added [VL.Captury](https://vvvv.org/blog/2026/introducing-support-for-captury/). 

Further we can report more progress on the Package Manager that should soon be ready for a merge to previews. And also we're finally continuing work on full support for the latest Orbbec depth cameras. Bare with us...
