# Lessons

## 2026-09-21

- A concrete benchmark must not be introduced with a vague condition that the benchmark already instantiates. State the measured setup directly and keep its scope explicit.
- Public announcement prose should describe the visible workflow or result. Remove implementation vocabulary such as DXR primitive types and internal example numbers unless the reader needs them to act.
- Lead the announcement with real-time path tracing as the capability. Keep NVIDIA RTXPT and other implementation details in the technical section rather than making them the subject of the opening.
- When compute is the point, state the general performance requirement and result in the opening. Put the exact benchmark hardware later as evidence instead of leading with its model name.
- Do not turn a GPU-dependent benchmark into a general frame-rate claim. Describe the durable capability and workflow, and omit the number when the hardware and scene determine the result.
- When a project has moved from announcement to release, re-read the latest forum update before revising the post. New capabilities and clean renders should replace the original teaser material.
- Keep the full hero image and the square `thumb` resource separate. The article uses the former in the body and the latter for the site's listing and social-preview pipeline.

## 2026-10-07

- For the first announcement of a graphics feature, describe the creative workflow before implementation mechanics. Keep buffers, shader-authored instancing and internal node evolution out unless they are necessary to understand what the reader can make.
- When the forum post contains the authoritative credits, carry the direct project and creator links into the technical paragraph instead of adding unrelated documentation links.
- Put installation in a short final section and keep hardware guidance as a durable general recommendation, not a benchmark claim.
- Keep the opening's superlative attached to the renderer and make it sound like an editorial judgment, not a universal performance guarantee. Remove secondary feature mentions the user has asked to cut, even if they are technically correct.
- Keep unapproved joke lines out of the article; present options separately when the user wants to choose the wording first.
